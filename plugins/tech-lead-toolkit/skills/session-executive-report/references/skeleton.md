# Session executive report skeleton

```
# Executive Report — <Session Title>
**Date:** <Month D, YYYY> · <HH:MM> UTC
**Duration:** ~<n> minutes
**Format:** <e.g., Microsoft Teams (recorded)>
**Prepared by:** <Given Surname, Role>
**Version:** <only when a version is assigned; see SKILL.md step 4>

---

## Executive Summary

<Paragraph 1: what was decided and why the session was convened. When nothing was decided, say so here.>

<Paragraph 2: the forward state. What work follows and in what sequence.>

<Paragraph 3: open governance questions and anything left unresolved, each with its owner where one exists.>

---

## Participants

| Name | Organization | Role / Track |
|---|---|---|
| <Given Surname> | <Org> | <Role, Track> |
| <Given Surname> | <Org> | <Role, Track> (invited; not transcribed) |

---

## Topics

### 1. <Topic Title>

**Background.** <What the topic is and why it came up.>

**Blocker Identified.** <If applicable: the blocker, its owner, what it blocks.>

**Resolution Path.** <What was agreed or proposed. Where positions conflict, both positions and the governing constraint.>

**Open Items.** <What remains.>

<Speaker Name>, <Role> [HH:MM:SS]
> "<verbatim quote>"

---

## Action Items

| # | Action | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <Action> | <Given Surname> | <Note> |

---

## References

| # | Resource | Live link |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | <Resource> | <URL> |
```

Rows in the Action Items table exist only for real commitments. A row with no owner means nobody committed; resolve it in the topic text instead.

## Revision note, when the report is versioned

```
**Revision note.** v0.2.0 (MINOR): adds Topic 4, omitted from the first issue. The decisions, owners, and dates recorded in v0.1.0 are unchanged.
```
